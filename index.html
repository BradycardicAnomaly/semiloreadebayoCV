Profile

Academic doctor with a strong teaching and research portfolio.

Doctor Oluwasemilore Adebayo is a medical doctor with an MBChB from the University of Birmingham, an MSc in Trauma Science with distinction, and a PGCert in Medical and Healthcare Education from the University of Hertfordshire.

His work spans trauma and orthopaedics, medical education, quality improvement, multicentre clinical trials, student mentorship and widening participation in medicine.
